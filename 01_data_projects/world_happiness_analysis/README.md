## Analyzing Global Well-being: World Happiness Report

### Objective
To explore the key factors influencing happiness across countries and regions using global development data from the World Happiness Report.

---

### Dataset
- Source: United Nations & Gallup World Poll  
- Variables analyzed:
  - GDP per capita  
  - Social support  
  - Life expectancy  
  - Freedom to make life choices  
  - Perception of corruption  
  - Positive and negative affect  

---

### Approach
- Data exploration and cleaning  
- Comparative analysis across regions  
- Visualization using maps, trend lines, and scatter plots  

---

### Key Insights
- Higher GDP per capita generally correlates with higher happiness levels  
- Social support and freedom are strong predictors of well-being  
- Regions show consistent disparities, with Europe and Oceania ranking higher than Africa  
- Perceived corruption negatively impacts happiness scores  

---

### Relevance
This project demonstrates how global datasets can be used to:
- understand well-being trends  
- support policy and governance decisions  
- identify regional disparities
