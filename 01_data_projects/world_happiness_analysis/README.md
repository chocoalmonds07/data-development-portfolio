## World Happiness Report Analysis

### Objective
To explore the factors associated with happiness across countries and regions using global development data.

---

### Project Context
This project was developed collaboratively as part of a data visualization course. It focused on using visual analytics to examine patterns in well-being and to communicate how economic and social indicators relate to happiness across different regions.

---

### Project Overview
Using data from the World Happiness Report, the project examined variables such as GDP per capita, social support, life expectancy, freedom, and corruption. The analysis used visualizations to compare countries and regions and identify broader development patterns.

---

### My Contribution
- Contributed to the interpretation of development-related indicators and regional patterns  
- Helped analyze how social and economic factors relate to happiness outcomes  
- Participated in presenting the findings through a clear visual storytelling structure  

The visualizations and broader analysis were developed collaboratively within the team.

---

### Key Features
- Regional and country-level comparisons of happiness scores  
- Visual analysis of relationships between happiness and key development indicators  
- Use of charts and maps to communicate global patterns and disparities  
- Framing of findings in relation to governance, development, and well-being
